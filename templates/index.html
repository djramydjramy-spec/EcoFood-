🔐 Comprehensive Food Safety and Compliance Protocol

Our safety is non-negotiable. Every partner pledges adherence to strict hygiene standards, including:

  • **Temperature Logs:** Hot foods must maintain safe temperatures, and cold foods must be quickly chilled and stored below 4°C.
  • **UBD/DLC Verification:** All products must have a clear, valid Use-By Date or be explicitly marked for same-day consumption.
  • **Packaging:** Products must be hygienically sealed or professionally packaged for safe transport and pickup.
  • **Allergen Disclosure:** Descriptions must explicitly detail all major allergens to protect public health.

Our community rating system facilitates real-time monitoring, ensuring rapid corrective action against non-compliant partners.

💡 The 5-Step Offer Lifecycle

  1. **1. Rapid Posting (The Offer):** The partner uses the `/add` interface to list the surplus (product, quantity, designated pickup time window).
  2. **2. Notification & Reservation:** Local users receive alerts. The user secures the offer via the platform to guarantee collection.
  3. **3. Token Generation:** A unique digital code (token) is generated, serving as proof of purchase or donation upon collection.
  4. **4. Verified Pickup:** The user presents the code, the partner verifies it, and the offer is marked "Sold Out" or "Donation Collected."
  5. **5. Impact Logging:** The action is automatically tallied in our central database, contributing to national impact statistics.
